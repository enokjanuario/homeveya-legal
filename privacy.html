Privacy Policy

HomeVeya · Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what data the HomeVeya app and website collect, why, and your choices. The data controller is Cameron Minaglia.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a hash); your name if you provide it.
  • Project data: DIY progress, paint colors, room measurements, maintenance tasks, storage catalog entries and saved contractors you create.
  • Photos: images you upload for photo analysis, room scanning and storage catalog entries. They are stored privately, with no public URL.
  • Usage data: the number of AI questions and photo analyses you use each month, to enforce plan limits.
  • Billing data: on the web: checkout, order and subscription IDs from our payment provider (Base44 Payments / Wix).
  • In-app purchase data: if you subscribe in the iOS or Android app, we receive your subscription status, product, store, expiration date and purchase history (transaction IDs) from the store through our subscription processor. We never receive your card or store account payment details.
  • Device identifiers: the app shell may process a device identifier needed for the app to work. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

2. How we use it

To provide the features you use (DIY guides, AI analysis, maintenance reminders, storage tracking, contractor directory), to manage your subscription and account, to contact you about your account and reminders you enabled, and to improve those features. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

3. Who we share it with

  • Base44: hosting, database and file storage.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: in-app subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat (subscription processor): processes in-app subscriptions on our behalf. We identify you to RevenueCat by your account email (used as your app user ID); it stores your purchase history and subscription status to verify purchases and keep your access in sync.
  • Payment provider (web): web checkout and subscription data is processed by Base44 Payments (Wix).
  • AI provider: your prompts and uploaded photos are sent to our AI provider to generate results.
  • Contractor directory: listings come from our database. External review links open third-party sites (Google, Yelp, etc.), governed by their own policies.

These providers process data only to deliver the service.

4. Retention

Your project data and photos are kept until you delete them or your account. Payment records (order IDs, amounts, dates) are kept in anonymized form after account deletion for tax and accounting compliance. Files in private storage may take additional time to be fully purged after account deletion; they are not accessible through the app once the referencing records are removed.

5. Your rights and account deletion

  • Access: you can view your data in the app at any time.
  • Delete: delete individual records, or your entire account in the app (Help > Delete account). You can also request deletion by emailing matheus.softwaress@gmail.com from your account address; we will delete your account and personal data. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription.
  • Subscriptions: cancel anytime. Paid access continues to the end of the billing period. Subscriptions bought in the app are canceled in the store: on iPhone, Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • Other requests: you may ask for access, correction, portability or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent, by email.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by row-level access controls: you can only see and modify your own records. Uploaded files are in private storage and accessed through time-limited signed URLs. We do not store payment card numbers.

7. Children

HomeVeya is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version.
