DIY or hire a pro? HomeVeya helps homeowners decide, then guides them through the project.

About the app

HomeVeya offers step-by-step DIY repair guides, AI home repair questions and photo analysis, painting planners and calculators, room measurements, a home maintenance schedule with email reminders, a storage catalog, how-to videos and a directory of home improvement professionals. It is available on iPhone, iPad and Android, and on the web.

Frequently asked questions

What do Pro and Pro Plus cost?

Pro is $4.99 per month and Pro Plus is $9.99 per month (US dollars; the store may show local prices). Both are auto-renewable subscriptions that renew monthly until canceled. The free plan stays available.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions bought in the app are managed by the store, not by us. On iPhone or iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore my purchases?

Sign in with the same account (email) you used when you subscribed, open the Plans screen and tap Restore Purchases. If access does not return, email us from your account address.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for purchases made in the iOS or Android app are handled by Apple or Google under their policies (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com; Google: Google Play order history). For other billing problems, email us within 14 days of the charge.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Help > Delete account. You can also email us from your account address and we will delete your account and data. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in the store first (see above).

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login screen to receive a reset email.
